## hello

The first program: it prints a greeting.

## fizzbuzz

The numbers from 1 to 100, with Fizz, Buzz and FizzBuzz for the multiples of 3, of 5 and of both.

## fibonacci

The 50th Fibonacci number, in a `u64`.

## primes

The sieve of Eratosthenes, up to 100.

## mandelbrot

The Mandelbrot set, drawn in characters.

## rule110

Rule 110, a one-dimensional cellular automaton, one row per generation.

## mowitnow

Lawn mowers driven across a grid: enums, a trait, an exhaustive `match`, and an input file that `@comptime` reads during the build.

## brainfuck

A Brainfuck compiler: it turns a program into the bytecode of the Vader VM and runs it, in memory.

## raycaster

A first-person walk through a hangar full of zombies. The engine draws every frame in Vader, pixel by pixel; raylib opens the window. Its output is that of the `frames` entry, which draws the game's views in characters.

## raylib_sprite

A window and a sprite that the arrow keys move, through raylib.
